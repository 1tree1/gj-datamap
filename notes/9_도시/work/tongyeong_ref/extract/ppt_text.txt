Sponge City
A sponge city is designed to passively 
absorb, clean and reuse rainfall in an 
ecologically friendly way that reduces the 
speed and pollution of runoff in the city.
Softening the Edge
Consider the waterfront as an 
ecosystem, improve its natural dynamics 
and reinforce its role in the city. 
Restore the Habitat
Naturally treat and remediate soil quality of the 
site.  Phytoremediation strategies to be used.
Supporting the Ecosystem
Creation of permeable features through 
the urban fabric. Creating an ecologically 
good place to be. Working with nature to 
come back stronger after disruptive 
natural events.
